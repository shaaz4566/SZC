SZC WEBSITES / WEBSITE MAINTENANCE

Support for your website after launch

Websites need occasional fixes, content updates and improvements. SZC Websites can discuss a maintenance arrangement suited to your site's platform, complexity and support needs.

The right support plan depends on how the website is built, who hosts it, how often it changes and how quickly issues need attention. We define the scope, access arrangements and response expectations for each project rather than promising an unsupported service level.

Maintenance work may include

  • Content and image updates
  • Bug investigation and small fixes
  • Dependency or platform update planning where applicable
  • Broken-link and basic page checks
  • Advice on backups, hosting and ongoing improvements

Every project is scoped around its actual goals, content, platform and integrations. Contact SZC Websites to discuss your requirements and confirm a suitable approach.
