08 / FAQ
Common questions

Questions about web design with SZC.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. Send your project details on WhatsApp and you get a fixed quote before any work starts.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site is built with SEO-ready titles, meta tags, structured data, a sitemap and fast loading, then submitted to Google Search Console. Rankings also depend on competition and content, and nobody can honestly guarantee the first position.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes. SZC registers your domain, sets up DNS and SSL, hosts the website and maintains it after launch.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. SZC works remotely with clients worldwide, over WhatsApp and video calls.

What do you build websites with?

HTML, CSS and JavaScript, which keeps pages fast, secure and easy to host. Web applications are built as browser-based software.
