01 / ABOUT SZC

SZC is a software design and development brand founded and developed by Shaaz Zakariya C.

SZC focuses on designing and building software, websites, digital products, and interactive experiences that combine technology, functionality, and modern visual design.

The brand brings together software design, programming, web development, UI/UX, and creative technology to create digital products that are simple, refined, responsive, and enjoyable to use.
