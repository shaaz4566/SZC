SZC WEBSITES / E-COMMERCE

An online store built around your products

We help plan and build e-commerce experiences that make products easier to explore and the buying journey easier to understand. The platform, payment methods, shipping rules and integrations depend on each project's requirements.

A strong online store needs more than a product grid. It needs understandable categories, useful product information, transparent policies and a checkout flow appropriate to the chosen platform. We agree the scope and technical approach before work begins.

E-commerce project considerations

  • Store structure and product category navigation
  • Product detail pages and product imagery
  • Cart and checkout experience based on the selected platform
  • Payment, shipping, tax and inventory requirements scoped per project
  • Responsive design and search-friendly page structure

Every project is scoped around its actual goals, content, platform and integrations. Contact SZC Websites to discuss your requirements and confirm a suitable approach.
